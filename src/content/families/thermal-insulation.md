As a ceramic fibre insulation manufacturer, we make four product types. Each one does a different job. Start with where the part will sit.

**Walls, roofs and doors.** Choose ceramic fibre boards. A board can serve as the hot face of a lining. It can also sit behind brick or castable as backup insulation.

**Pipes and ducts.** Choose pipe sections. They fit around a hot pipe to cut heat loss and keep the outside cooler. We need the pipe outer diameter and the thickness you want.

**Joints and seals.** Choose gaskets. They seal furnace doors, flanges, burner mounts and access covers. Send the outer size, inner size and bolt hole pattern.

**Burner openings.** Choose burner shapes. Blocks and quarls line the opening and guide the flame into the chamber. We make them to your burner and wall details.

For every product, tell us the operating temperature where it will be fitted. This helps us suggest the right grade and density.

If your part does not fit these four types, we make custom ceramic fibre shapes to your drawing. Send a drawing, a sample or the dimensions with the application. We will quote for the part you need.

Hot gas filtration ceramic candles clean dust from gas while it is still hot. Choosing the right candle starts with your filter housing and your gas.

First, check the housing. For a replacement, we need the candle length, outer diameter and flange detail. A used candle or the housing drawing gives us all three. For a new filter, share the housing drawing and the number of candles.

Next, look at the gas. Tell us the gas temperature at the filter and the type of dust. Mention anything in the gas that may attack the candle, such as acids or alkalis. These details help us suggest the right grade.

Then think about how the filter is cleaned. Most candle filters use a reverse pulse of air. Tell us your cleaning method and cycle, so the candle suits the load it will take.

Finally, plan your quantity. Many plants keep a set of spare candles on site for planned shutdowns. Tell us your monthly or yearly need, and we can plan supply around it.

If you are not sure whether candles or bag filters suit your line, send us your process details. We will tell you plainly whether our candles fit your conditions.

As a foundry consumables manufacturer in India, we make five products that cover the path from melt to mould. Each one does a different job, so the choice follows the step in your process.

**Checking the melt.** A sampling spoon takes a quick sample from the furnace or ladle. Use it when you need a clean sample for the lab before you pour.

**Tapping the furnace.** A tap-out cone seals the tap hole between taps. Choose it for a reliable seal and a clean release when you tap.

**Melting and holding.** A crucible holds the charge while it melts and carries the melt to the pour. Choose the size that fits your furnace and the metal you melt.

**Pouring into the mould.** A pouring cup sits on the sprue and takes the metal from the ladle. It gives the pourer a steady, controlled stream.

**Feeding the casting.** A feeder sleeve keeps the feeder liquid for longer. Insulating sleeves slow heat loss. Exothermic sleeves also add heat. Both help with yield and sounder castings.

Most foundries buy more than one of these. Send us the products, sizes and monthly quantities together, and we will quote them as one order. If your part is not a standard size, send a drawing or a sample and we will make it to match.

We supply ceramic fibre products for non-ferrous foundries. Our range covers the parts your shop uses to melt, tap and pour the metal every day.

Crucibles hold the metal while it melts in the furnace, and can be made to your drawing. Tap-out cones seal the tap hole and release when you tap. Pouring cups guide metal into the mould with less spill.

We make standard sizes and parts to your drawing, in our own plant in Hyderabad. Tell us the metal you melt, your furnace type and your monthly quantity. We will suggest the right product and send a quote.

We supply ceramic fibre products for furnace and kiln builders. Our range covers the insulation parts that go into a new build, a reline or a repair.

Ceramic fibre boards line and back up walls, roofs and doors. Ceramic fibre gaskets seal doors, flanges and ports. Burner shapes fit around the burner and guide the flame into the chamber.

When a standard part does not fit, we make custom shapes to your drawing. Every part is made in our own plant in Hyderabad. Send your drawing or parts list, with the operating temperature. We will quote for the full set.

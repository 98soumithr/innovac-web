We supply ceramic fibre products for iron foundries that melt grey iron and SG iron. Our range covers the consumables a melt shop and moulding line use every shift.

Sampling spoons take a clean sample for the lab. Tap-out cones seal the tap hole and release when you tap. Pouring cups guide metal into the mould. Insulating and exothermic sleeves keep feeders liquid for longer, so castings feed properly.

We make standard sizes and parts to your drawing, in our own plant in Hyderabad. Tell us your metal, your pouring practice and your monthly quantity. We will suggest the right product and send a quote.

We supply ceramic fibre products for steel plants and steel foundries. Our range covers sampling, feeding and heat insulation, from the melt shop to the ladle bay.

Sampling spoons let the melt shop take a sample from the furnace or ladle for analysis. Insulating and exothermic sleeves help steel castings feed through the feeder. Ceramic fibre boards line and back up ladle covers, furnace walls and hot equipment to cut heat loss.

We make standard sizes and parts to your drawing, in our own plant in Hyderabad. Tell us the steel grade, your pouring practice and the quantity you use. We will suggest the right product and send a quote.

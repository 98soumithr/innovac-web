We supply ceramic fibre products for glass and cement plants, and for other process plants that handle hot gas. The work falls into two ranges.

Our hot gas filtration range is built around ceramic filter candles. Candles take dust out of hot gas streams, so the gas can be cleaned without cooling it first.

Our thermal insulation range covers ceramic fibre boards, pipe sections and gaskets. They cut heat loss from ducts, pipes, kilns and furnace doors.

We make standard sizes and parts to your drawing, in our own plant in Hyderabad. Tell us your gas, your dust and your operating temperature. We will suggest the right product and send a quote.

A ceramic fibre pipe section wraps around a hot pipe or duct. It cuts heat loss along the line and keeps the outer surface cooler for people working nearby.

We make pipe sections to fit the outer diameter of your pipe. You choose the wall thickness and section length. They are made in our Hyderabad plant.

Pipe sections go onto hot gas ducts, flue lines, process pipework and exhaust lines.

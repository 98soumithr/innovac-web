A sampling spoon lets your melt shop take a quick, clean sample of molten metal for analysis. The result tells you whether the chemistry is right before the metal goes into the mould.

Our spoons are light to handle and ready to use from the carton. They are made in our Hyderabad plant in standard sizes, or to the bowl and handle dimensions you already use.

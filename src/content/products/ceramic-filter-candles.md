Ceramic filter candles remove dust from hot process gas and flue gas. The gas passes through the candle wall, and the dust collects on the outside. You can clean the gas while it is still hot, without cooling it first.

We make candles to your length, diameter and flange detail. We supply them for new filter housings and as replacements for candles already in service. They are made in our Hyderabad plant.

Our candles go into glass furnaces, cement and lime kilns, biomass plants, boilers, incinerators and metal processing off-gas lines.

As a ceramic fibre board manufacturer in India, we make rigid boards for furnace and kiln linings. A board can take the heat directly on the hot face. It can also sit behind brick or castable as backup insulation.

Boards hold their shape and are easy to cut on site. They cut heat loss through walls, roofs and doors, so the shell runs cooler.

We supply boards in standard sizes or cut to your drawing. They are made in our Hyderabad plant.

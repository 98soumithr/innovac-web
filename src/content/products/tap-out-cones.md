We are a tap out cone manufacturer in Hyderabad, supplying foundries across India. A tap-out cone seals the furnace tap hole between taps. It holds back the metal until the melter is ready to pour.

When it is time to tap, the cone knocks out cleanly. The metal flows without a fight at the tap hole, and the next cone goes in quickly.

Our cones are made in our Hyderabad plant in standard sizes, or to the diameter and taper of your tap hole.

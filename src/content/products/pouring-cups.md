A pouring cup for foundry use sits on top of the sprue and takes the metal from the ladle. It gives the pourer a larger target and a steady stream into the mould.

A controlled pour means less splash at the mould top and a cleaner fill of the running system. Our cups are light to place and ready to use from the carton.

We make them in our Hyderabad plant in standard sizes, or to the cup and outlet dimensions you already use.

Our burner block ceramic fibre shapes line the openings where burners fire into a furnace or kiln. They protect the wall around the burner and guide the flame into the chamber.

We make blocks and quarls to suit your burner and your wall. Send us the bore profile, outer size and thickness, or a drawing. They are made in our Hyderabad plant.

Burner shapes are light, so they are easy to fit and replace during a shutdown.

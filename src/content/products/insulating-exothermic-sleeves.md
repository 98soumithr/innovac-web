As an exothermic sleeves manufacturer in India, we make insulating and exothermic feeder sleeves for foundries. A sleeve lines the feeder, or riser, on top of the casting.

It keeps the metal in the feeder liquid for longer. The feeder can then do its job and fill the casting as it shrinks. The result is sounder castings, smaller feeders and better yield from each pour.

Insulating sleeves slow heat loss. Exothermic sleeves also add heat. We make both in our Hyderabad plant, in standard sizes or to your feeder dimensions.

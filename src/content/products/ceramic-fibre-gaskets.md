A ceramic fibre gasket seals a joint that runs hot. It stops heat and hot gas from escaping around furnace doors, flanges, burner mounts and access covers.

We cut gaskets to your drawing or to a sample you send. Tell us the outer size, inner size, thickness and bolt hole pattern. They are made in our Hyderabad plant.

A good seal keeps heat inside the furnace. It also keeps the area around the joint cooler and safer.
